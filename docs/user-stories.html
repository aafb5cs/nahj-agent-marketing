End to end

One sale, start to finish

  1. 1SellerCreates a sale link

    Yearly × 10, codes. Shares the link or QR on WhatsApp.

  2. 2BuyerSigns in and confirms

    Nahj account, quote in IDR, terms accepted. The order is created.

  3. 3BuyerPays by QRIS or VA

    The provider is chosen by route. The webhook is verified.

  4. 4SystemFulfills instantly

    10 codes appear in My Purchases. A direct order adds VIP time right away.

  5. 5RecipientRedeems a code

    On the web or in Android. VIP days start now, added to the end.

  6. 6SystemReleases commission

    Per unit, after 168 h and redemption or the 30-day end.

  7. 7SellerWithdraws

    Min Rp500,000, TOTP step-up, Finance queue, payout to bank.

  8. 8Buyer · FinanceRefunds unused codes

    Within 30 days. Commission on those units is reversed.

  9. BAgent · UserOr: app purchase with agent code

    User enters AGT-7K2M3P in the app and buys in the store. The agent earns on net after the hold.
